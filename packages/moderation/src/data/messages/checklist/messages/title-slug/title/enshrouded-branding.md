## Enshrouded Project Names

Projects must not use Enshrouded's branding or include "Enshrouded" as a significant part of the title. \
Your project's current Name of `%PROJECT_TITLE%` may be confusingly similar to, or imply association with, the game Enshrouded. We encourage you to change your project's [Name](%PROJECT_SETTINGS_LINK%) to avoid a potential violation of Enshrouded's Usage Guidelines. \
Abbreviations like "MC" or elaborate titles that do not make the name Enshrouded a significant portion of the name are okay. \
When editing your project's Name, remember to update its [URL](%PROJECT_SETTINGS_LINK%) to match.
