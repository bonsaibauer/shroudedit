Hey folks!

I’m **bonsaibauer**, and I’m incredibly happy to welcome you to ShroudEdit!

This project has been quite a journey. What started as a small hobby project eventually grew into **ShroudForge**, a mod loader for Enshrouded and a successor to EML. The goal was to make it easier to manage mods and their settings directly in the game, while continuing to support existing EML mods.

But thanks to the wonderful Enshrouded modding community, things didn’t stop there. More people joined in, shared their ideas, helped each other, and inspired me to keep building. Along the way, [shroudforge.net](https://shroudforge.net/) and the [ShroudForge Wiki](https://wiki.shroudforge.net/) came to life.

And then a question came up: **What if Enshrouded had its own community platform, like Modrinth for Minecraft?**

That idea became ShroudEdit.

## A home for your Enshrouded creations

ShroudEdit is a place to discover and share mods, modpacks, schematics, and community servers. I want it to be a welcoming home for everyone who enjoys creating, exploring, and making Enshrouded their own.

ShroudEdit is built on the open-source software behind [Modrinth](https://github.com/modrinth/code). A huge thank you to the Modrinth team and its contributors for making their work available to the community. 💚

One of the ideas behind ShroudEdit was to connect the platform with tools like ShroudForge. Its API makes it possible for a mod loader to find mods and check for updates, helping players keep their mods up to date. Uploaded files are also checked for malware to help make downloading safer.

## Share a piece of your world

Another idea grew out of conversations with the community and the early work on the World Editor: What if you could copy a house or another part of your world and share it with others?

That’s where **schematics**, also known as blueprints, come in. You can upload your creations to ShroudEdit for other players to discover and download, then place them in their own Enshrouded worlds using the World Editor.

I can’t wait to see what you build and share!

## A little patience as we get started

ShroudEdit is only the beginning. I don’t know yet how many people will visit or how the service will handle the initial demand, so there may be a few bumps along the way. Moderation and responses might also take a little longer at first.

Please bear with me while I learn, improve things, and help the platform grow. Your patience means a lot. 💚

## Come say hello

Whether you’re a player, a modder, a builder, or someone who has simply noticed something that could be better, **I’d love to hear from you**. Your ideas and feedback have already helped shape this project, and I’m excited to see where we can take it together.

For general questions and quick help, come join us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/ppzyYaVqeA). If you need help with the website, your account, your data, or your files, please email [support@shroudedit.com](mailto:support@shroudedit.com).

Thank you for being such a kind and supportive community. Without you, your creativity, and your encouragement, ShroudEdit wouldn’t be here.

Have fun exploring, creating, and sharing!
